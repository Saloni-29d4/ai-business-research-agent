# 🤖 AI Business Research & Competitive Intelligence Agent

An Agentic AI system designed to transform strategic business questions into structured, evidence-based research briefs.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Business decision-makers often need to collect information from multiple sources, compare competing information, verify evidence, and identify contradictions before making strategic decisions.

This project demonstrates an Agentic AI workflow that organizes these research activities into specialized agents.

## 💡 Our Solution

The system takes a strategic business question as input and processes it through a multi-agent research pipeline.

The final output separates information into:

- ✅ Verified Facts
- ⚠️ Conflicting Information
- 🧠 Inferences
- ❓ Unknown Information

This helps decision-makers understand not only the available evidence, but also where uncertainty remains.
